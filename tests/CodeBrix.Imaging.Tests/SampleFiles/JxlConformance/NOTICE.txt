The bike and cafe bitstreams and previews are NOT included. Their tests
require the explicit external-test environment gate before downloading them.
Full floating-point, ICC and reconstructed JPEG references are likewise
external and SHA-256 verified. Their hashes remain in upstream test.json.

Level 5 cases share base-case inputs/previews where upstream does so.
manifest.json retains all 24 Level 5 and 27 Level 10 test records. Metadata
and licenses count toward the aggregate 100,000,000-byte resource budget.
